## Back-cover copy

### English

The hardest part is rarely deciding that screens need a boundary. It is holding that boundary at 7:42 p.m., when everyone is tired and the next school morning is already getting closer.

The 7-Day School-Night Screen Reset turns that moment into a plan. Choose one boundary, explain it without a lecture, use the scripts when pushback arrives, and track what actually happens for seven nights. You will get age-adjusted routines, a family agreement, screen-free replacement ideas, and a troubleshooting map for the nights that go sideways.

This is not a device ban or a parenting test. It is a practical way to make the next school night clearer than the last one.

### Spanish

The 7-Day School-Night Screen Reset convierte ese momento en un plan. Elige un límite, explícalo sin dar un sermón, usa las frases cuando aparezca la resistencia y observa lo que ocurre durante siete noches. Incluye rutinas por edad, un acuerdo familiar, alternativas sin pantalla y un mapa para ajustar las noches que se complican.

No es una prohibición de dispositivos ni una prueba de crianza. Es una forma práctica de hacer que la próxima noche escolar sea más clara que la anterior.

## Cover brief

The cover should feel like a calm kitchen-table planning tool, not a clinical manual and not a childish reward chart. Use a warm paper ground, deep ink blue, muted coral, sage, and a small mustard accent. The central motif is a seven-step path ending at a small device-parking tray. Use strong editorial typography, generous negative space, and simple geometric illustrations. The title must remain legible at 100 pixels.

Avoid photographs of distressed children, glowing phones, warning symbols, brain imagery, neon gradients, medical blue, cartoon mascots, and fake seals of approval.

## Sales synopsis

The product is a compact implementation system for parents of children ages 6-12. It begins with a ten-minute quick start, then helps the reader separate necessary from recreational use, select one school-night boundary, plan a replacement activity, and hold a short family meeting. Seven daily pages create a progressive sequence from observation to adjustment and maintenance. Scripts cover advance warnings, stopping, pushback, exceptions, and caregiver disagreement. Two age-adjusted routine builders, a device-parking plan, a family agreement, a tracker, replacement menus, repair guidance, and a troubleshooting map complete the kit.

Its market difference is speed-to-action. It does not compete with research-heavy parenting books on depth. It translates the most usable principles from family media planning into a printable workflow a parent can use the same evening.
